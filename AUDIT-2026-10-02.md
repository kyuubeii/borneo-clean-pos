# Audit — Borneo Clean, 2 Oct 2026

A reviewer's read of the server actions, API routes, web pages and the iPhone
screens that do their own sums, against commit `6f0ef7e`. It follows up
[LOGIC-AUDIT.md](LOGIC-AUDIT.md) (20 Sep).

**Nothing in the data was changed.** Every live-data check went through
`/api/actions/batch`, which refuses anything that is not read-only.

The live data is clean today. No cancelled job has an open invoice, no invoice
is overpaid, none is marked paid without the money, no payroll period was paid
twice, and every booking has its job. So almost everything below is a door left
open, not damage already done.

- **A–D: cleaners.**
  - Only their own jobs.
  - Only On the way, In progress and Complete. Cancelling and rescheduling stay with the office.
  - Check in only as themselves, and never to a closed job.
  - Colleagues show as name and colour only.
  - The job page no longer carries anyone's pay, the invoice or expenses.
  - Owners and admins are unchanged.
- **E–F:** Refunds are capped at what was paid, and a void invoice takes no payment.

  RM 330 not invoiced (JOB-0041, 0043, 0049, 0055).

## Q3: why the dashboard revenue chart is flat

The chart plots **cash collected by payment date**, not sales. October has
nothing collected yet, so it is flat. September shows the difference between the
two: RM 4,070 collected (some of it August's work) against RM 4,000 of sales.
